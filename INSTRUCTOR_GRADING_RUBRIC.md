# Instructor Grading Rubric

This rubric provides detailed evaluation criteria for assessing student performance in the 12-week Call Automation Capstone Project.

---

## Overall Grade Distribution

| Phase | Component | Weight | Points |
|-------|-----------|--------|--------|

| 2 | Development Phase I (Weeks 3-5) | 20% | 200 |
| 3 | Development Phase II (Weeks 6-8) | 25% | 250 |
| 4 | Testing & Quality Assurance (Weeks 9-10) | 15% | 150 |
| 5 | Finalization & Presentation (Weeks 11-12) | 20% | 200 |
| 6 | Code Quality & Best Practices (Continuous) | 10% | 100 |
| **Total** | | **100%** | **1000** |

## Individual Assessment Modifiers

### Peer Evaluation (Can adjust individual grades by ±10%)

Students complete peer evaluations assessing:
- **Contribution Level:** Equal, above average, or below average
- **Reliability:** Meeting deadlines and commitments
- **Communication:** Team meetings and updates
- **Technical Contribution:** Code quality and problem-solving
- **Teamwork:** Helping others and collaboration

### Attendance & Participation (Can affect grade by ±5%)

- Weekly meeting attendance
- Office hours attendance when needed
- Class participation and questions
- Helping other teams/students

---

## Bonus Opportunities (Up to 50 Extra Points)

Students can earn bonus points for:
- **Innovation** (up to 20 points): Implementing creative features beyond requirements
- **Open Source Contribution** (up to 15 points): Contributing improvements back to the Azure samples
- **Documentation Excellence** (up to 10 points): Creating additional helpful guides or tutorials
- **Community Engagement** (up to 5 points): Presenting at student events, writing blog posts

## Weekly Grading Workflow

### For Instructors

1. **Monday:** Review previous week's submissions
2. **Tuesday:** Grade deliverables using rubrics
3. **Wednesday:** Provide feedback to teams
4. **Thursday:** Hold office hours for questions
5. **Friday:** Attend team demos and retrospectives

### Grading Tips

- **Be Consistent:** Use the rubric strictly for fairness
- **Provide Feedback:** Specific comments help students learn
- **Grade Promptly:** Return grades within 48 hours
- **Document Issues:** Note common problems for class discussion
- **Recognize Excellence:** Highlight exceptional work publicly (with permission)

---

## Common Grading Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Team Member Not Contributing
- Document the issue
- Review peer evaluations
- Meet with individual student
- May assign individual tasks for assessment
- Can adjust individual grade separately from team

### Scenario 2: Technical Difficulties Beyond Control
- Azure service outages
- Phone number availability issues
- Consider extensions or alternative assignments
- Document circumstances

### Scenario 3: Outstanding Work
- Recognize publicly
- Consider for bonus points
- Recommend for showcase/portfolio
- May serve as example for future students

### Scenario 4: Academic Integrity Concerns
- Document suspicions
- Check code similarity tools
- Follow institutional policies
- May require individual oral examination

## Self-Assessment Forms

Provide students with self-assessment forms for each phase:
- What did you learn?
- What challenges did you face?
- How did you overcome them?
- What would you do differently?
- How did you contribute to the team?

Compare self-assessments with actual performance to gauge self-awareness.
